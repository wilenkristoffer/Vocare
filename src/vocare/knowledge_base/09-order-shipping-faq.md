# Orders & Shipping FAQ (Parts and Canisters)

## Ordering replacement canisters

Canisters (empty, for loading with medication on-site) are ordered through the customer portal
under Supplies > Canisters. Standard lead time is 3-5 business days.

## Ordering consumables

Label rolls, cleaning wipes, and calibration weight sets are also ordered through the customer
portal, under Supplies > Consumables. These typically ship within 1-2 business days and most
sites keep a rolling stock rather than ordering per-need.

## Expedited shipping

Available on the same portal for an extra fee, cutting standard lead times roughly in half.
Not available for calibration weight sets, which are always standard shipping due to
carrier handling requirements for the weighted contents.

## Tracking an order

Order status and tracking are both visible in the customer portal under Orders > Order
History. There's no separate tracking number lookup outside the portal.

## Damaged or missing items on arrival

Report through the customer portal's Orders > Report an Issue flow within 14 days of delivery.
Replacements for verified shipping damage ship at no charge.
