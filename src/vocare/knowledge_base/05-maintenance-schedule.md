# Maintenance Schedule

## Daily (pharmacy staff)

- Wipe down external surfaces and the touchscreen.
- Empty the reject/waste tray.
- Visual check: no visible tablet dust buildup around dispensing heads.

## Weekly (pharmacy staff)

- Clean weight sensors and dispensing head chutes (see cleaning procedures).
- Check label printer for dust and residue.

## Monthly (pharmacy staff)

- Routine calibration of all heads (see calibration).
- Review the unit's error log for recurring codes even if each one was individually resolved -
  a code that keeps recurring is a signal something needs a technician even if each instance
  seemed minor.

## Every 6 months (technician visit)

- Full internal cleaning.
- Sensor and motor inspection.
- Firmware/hardware health check.

## Annually (technician visit)

- Weight sensor recertification against a traceable reference standard.
- Full mechanical inspection of dispensing heads and canister slots.
