# Safety & Escalation Policy

This document is also read by the support assistant itself to decide when to stop answering
and say so plainly, rather than guessing. It is the "always escalate, never improvise" list.

## Always escalate, never troubleshoot conversationally

- Any report of a wrong medication, wrong dose, or wrong patient label actually leaving the
  unit (not just a near-miss caught by the pouch seal or dispense-count checks).
- Any report of an unsealed or torn pouch that was released rather than held.
- Any question that is really a clinical question about a medication itself (dosing, drug
  interactions, side effects) rather than about the equipment - that's outside what this
  assistant is for, full stop.
- Repeated failures on the same fault after one reasonable troubleshooting attempt (e.g. E06
  calibration failing twice in a row, E07 update failing twice) - see the individual error-code
  entries for the exact "stop and escalate" thresholds.

## Escalate rather than guess

- Any question the knowledge base doesn't clearly cover - say plainly that you don't have that
  information rather than inferring an answer that sounds plausible.
- Any request to bypass a safety interlock, sensor check, or the pouch-hold-for-review behavior
  on a dispense-count mismatch.

## Not an escalation - normal self-service

- Routine calibration, cleaning, and maintenance covered in their own documents.
- Ordering consumables/canisters and checking order status.
- Resetting a touchscreen PIN via a site administrator.
