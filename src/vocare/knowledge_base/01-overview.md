# Meridian AutoDose Overview

## What it is

The Meridian AutoDose is a benchtop robotic dispensing unit used in hospital and pharmacy
back-rooms to count, sort, and package oral solid medication (tablets and capsules) into
patient-specific pouches. It reads a prescription order from the pharmacy system, pulls the
correct medication canisters, dispenses the exact count, and prints a labeled pouch.

## Models

- **AutoDose 200** - single dispensing head, up to 60 canisters, small/independent pharmacies.
- **AutoDose 500** - dual dispensing head, up to 120 canisters, hospital pharmacies.
- **AutoDose 500 XL** - AutoDose 500 plus a high-volume bulk hopper for the 20 highest-turnover
  medications.

## Typical daily workflow

1. Pharmacist reviews and approves the order in the pharmacy system.
2. Order is sent to the AutoDose unit over the local network.
3. Unit dispenses and seals the pouch, printing patient name, drug, dose, and time on the label.
4. Pharmacist does a final visual check before the pouch leaves the pharmacy.
