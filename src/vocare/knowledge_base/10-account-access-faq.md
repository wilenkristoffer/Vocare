# Account & Access FAQ

## Touchscreen user accounts vs. customer portal accounts

These are two separate systems: the unit's touchscreen has its own local staff accounts
(for pausing/resuming dispensing, running calibration, viewing the error log), while the
customer portal (web) is used for ordering supplies and managing site-level settings. Resetting
one does not affect the other.

## Resetting a touchscreen PIN

A site administrator (a designated staff role, not necessarily a pharmacist) can reset any
staff member's touchscreen PIN from Settings > Staff Accounts on the unit itself. If no site
administrator is available or the admin account's own PIN is lost, that requires verified
support contact - it can't be self-served from the touchscreen for security reasons.

## Customer portal password reset

Self-service via the "Forgot password" link on the portal login page, sent to the account's
registered email.

## Adding or removing staff accounts

Site administrators manage touchscreen staff accounts locally on the unit. Customer portal
user management (who can place orders, who can view invoices) is separate and managed under
Portal Settings > Users by whoever holds the portal's owner/admin role for the site.

## Multiple units at one site

Each AutoDose unit has its own local touchscreen accounts, but all units at a site typically
share one customer portal account/site profile for ordering and billing.
