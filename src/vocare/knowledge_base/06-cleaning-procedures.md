# Cleaning Procedures

## Before you start

Always pause the unit (Settings > Pause Dispensing) before cleaning, and use only the
manufacturer-supplied cleaning wipes - other solvents can damage sensor housings and are not
covered by warranty if they cause a fault.

## External surfaces and touchscreen

Wipe with a manufacturer cleaning wipe. Avoid spraying liquid directly on the touchscreen.

## Weight sensors

1. Pause dispensing and remove the canister from the slot being cleaned.
2. Use the small brush (supplied) to clear visible tablet dust from the sensor tray.
3. Wipe the sensor tray with a dry manufacturer wipe - do not use a wet wipe on the sensor
   contacts.
4. Reseat the canister and resume dispensing.

## Dispensing head chutes

1. Pause dispensing.
2. Open the chute access panel (tool-free, thumb latch).
3. Brush out any dust or fragments.
4. Close the panel fully until it clicks before resuming - the unit won't dispense from that
   head if the panel isn't seated, by design.

## What not to do

- Don't use compressed air near the weight sensors - it can drive dust further into the sensor
  rather than out.
- Don't clean while an order is mid-dispense.
