# Calibration

## When to calibrate

- After any E06 "calibration drift detected" error.
- After moving the unit, even a short distance.
- On the schedule in the maintenance document (routine calibration).
- After replacing a dispensing head or weight sensor.

## Routine calibration procedure

1. From the unit's touchscreen, go to Settings > Calibration.
2. Remove all canisters from the head being calibrated.
3. Load the calibration weight set (supplied with the unit) when prompted.
4. The unit runs a short automatic sequence (about 3 minutes) and reports pass/fail per slot.
5. Re-load canisters only after calibration reports "pass" for that head.

## If calibration fails

- A single failed slot: try cleaning the slot's weight sensor (see cleaning procedures) and
  recalibrate once.
- A failed slot that fails twice in a row, or more than two slots failing: stop, do not keep
  retrying - this points to a hardware issue and needs a technician visit.

## Who can calibrate

Routine calibration is a normal pharmacy-staff task and doesn't require a technician. Anything
involving opening the unit's housing or replacing a part does require a technician.
