# Warranty & Support Tiers

## Standard warranty

2 years from installation date, covers parts and technician labor for hardware faults not
caused by misuse, unapproved cleaning products, or unauthorized modification.

## Support tiers

- **Standard** - included with every unit. Business-hours support, next-business-day
  technician dispatch for hardware issues.
- **Priority** - paid add-on. Extended-hours support, same-day technician dispatch, and a loaner
  unit if a repair will take longer than 48 hours.
- **Priority Plus** - paid add-on, hospital sites only. Everything in Priority, plus a
  dedicated on-call technician contact and a guaranteed 4-hour on-site response for
  patient-safety-flagged issues.

## What voids warranty coverage on a specific fault

- Using non-manufacturer cleaning products where damage results (see cleaning procedures).
- Opening the unit's housing without a technician present.
- Continuing to operate a unit after a patient-safety issue was identified (see the safety and
  escalation policy) instead of pausing and escalating.

## Checking your site's current tier

Visible in the customer portal under Account > Support Plan, or on the touchscreen under
Settings > About > Support Tier.
